WHAT WE DO

Our Services

Textile Graphic Designing

Creative and trend-focused graphic designs developed specifically for garments, including kidswear, casualwear, sportswear, fashion collections, and export apparel.

Screen Print Colour Separation

Professional colour separation for screen printing with accurate spot colours, clean layers, registration-ready files, and production-friendly artwork.

Digital Artwork Creation

High-quality digital artworks created or refined from concepts, sketches, references and customer requirements, ready for garment printing and production.

Photo Print & CMYK Separation

Detailed photo-print artwork preparation with professional CMYK separation, tonal control, colour balancing, and print-ready output for textile applications.

DTF Artwork

DTF-optimized artwork prepared with sharp details, correct sizing, clean edges, suitable colour treatment, and production-ready files for high-quality garment transfers.

Production-Ready Garment Designs

Complete print artworks prepared according to actual garment production requirements, including correct dimensions, placements, colours, separations, and final printable files.
